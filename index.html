How the math works

  • MA3 / MA7 / MA14 / MA28 / MA56 / MA112: averages of entries dated within the last N calendar days relative to your view as-of date (B).
  • Prior: the immediately preceding calendar window of the same length.
  • Δ: current average minus prior average (positive means upward trend).
  • If you skip a day, it is simply not counted (no zeros inserted).
  • Maintenance est.: average calories eaten over the window, minus the calories your weight change accounts for (trend change × 3500 ÷ window days). The trend uses MA7 at each end of the window, so daily water noise stays out. It calibrates to your logging, so consistency matters more than accuracy — but it needs calories on at least 80% of the window's days.
  • Latest day's balance: that day's calories minus the maintenance estimate — your surplus (+) or deficit (−), with the weekly pace it implies.
  • Today's calorie budget: maintenance, adjusted for today's steps vs your average, plus the Plan's catch-up pace converted to calories (pace × 3500 ÷ 7). Behind pace → the budget tightens; ahead → it loosens.
  • Base burn + per-step (fit): finds the base-burn and kcal-per-step pair whose predicted weight curve (eaten − burned, day by day) best matches your actual trend. The average alone can't split base from steps — the day-to-day variation in your steps is what identifies it, so the fit declines (with a reason) until the data supports it. When valid, the budget uses the fitted per-step value; otherwise the manual fallback.
